My take: The honest pick is B (Linear-minimal) — it's the visual language modern dev-tool dashboards have converged on for a reason: it gets out of the way. C is the second-best if you want it to feel more "ops engineer" / less "SaaS product". A is a brand surface, not a daily workspace — it could become the marketing screenshot or an optional "Atlas mode" toggle in v1.x. D is overkill until we actually have telemetry data to show.
